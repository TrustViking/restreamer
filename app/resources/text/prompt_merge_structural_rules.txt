STRUCTURAL RULES — APPLY TO EVERY MERGE OUTPUT

RULE 1 — NO CTA AS OPENER:
The first paragraph MUST be the editorial hook.
It must NEVER be a call to action, engagement request, or subscription prompt.
FORBIDDEN first-paragraph starts:
"Підпишіться" / "Підписуйтесь" / "Подпишитесь" / "Подписывайтесь" /
"Subscribe" / "Follow" / "Join" / "Watch" / "Поширюйте" / "Поділіться" /
"Приєднуйтесь" / "Смотрите" / "Следите"
Do not write a CTA at all. The first paragraph must be the editorial hook.
If you place a CTA as the first paragraph, the output WILL be rejected.

RULE — NO COMMENT CTA:
Do not include any calls to action asking viewers to leave comments,
write comments, or share thoughts in comments.
Examples of prohibited phrases: "Leave a comment", "Write in the comments",
"Напишіть у коментарях", "Оставляйте комментарии", "Залиште коментар".
This applies to all languages and all positions in the description
(hook, body, closing paragraph, CTA block).

RULE 2 — HOOK UNIQUENESS:
The hook paragraph appears exactly ONCE — as the first paragraph.
Paragraph 2 and all subsequent content must move FORWARD with new facts.
FORBIDDEN patterns:
- Restating the hook question in paragraph 2 (even rephrased)
- Opening the bullet block with a sentence that echoes the hook
- Writing the same thesis twice with different wording
If the hook is "Як так вийшло, що X?", paragraph 2 must NOT begin
with "Як так вийшло..." or any paraphrase. It must start with a bullet
marker (🔹, ⚖, 📌, etc.) and a NEW fact.

RULE 3 — BULLET COUNT DISCIPLINE:
The number of bullets must stay within the range specified by the contract.
If the contract says 4-7 bullets, writing 8 or more WILL be rejected.
If the contract says 5-7, writing 8 or more WILL be rejected.
Do not pad with generic bullets. Each bullet = one distinct fact or angle.

RULE 4 — PARAGRAPH 2 STARTS WITH A BULLET:
Immediately after the hook, the next content must be the bullet block.
Paragraph 2 must begin with a bullet marker, not with another prose sentence,
not with a rephrased hook, not with a promotional phrase.

EXAMPLE — CORRECT structure (uk):
⚖ Суд відкинув «експертні висновки» як неналежні докази у справі руху «АЛЛАТРА» — і це змінює правила гри.
🔹 Резонансна судова справа та її значення як прецеденту.
🔹 Аналіз: чому «експертні висновки» не відповідають вимогам належних доказів.
⚖ Факти про діяльність Кременовської та Святогора.
🔹 Зв'язок із риторикою РАЦИРС та наслідки для Європи.
